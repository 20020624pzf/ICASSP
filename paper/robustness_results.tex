\noindent\textbf{Exploratory fitting comparison.} MWER gives lower test point estimates than ridge, but both MWER--ridge contrasts include zero even in ordinary 95\% CV27 intervals (Fig.~\ref{fig:contrasts}(c) shows the adjusted intervals). On DEV, MWER-Global is worse (19.6975 versus 19.6864), while MWER-Conf improves (19.8137 versus 19.8724). Within MWER, Global improves over Conf by 0.1316 FLEURS and 0.2039 CV27 points. Both loss and weighting change.

\noindent\textbf{Selection behavior.} On seed101 CV27, Global lowers the edit count for 4{,}272 recordings and raises it for 3{,}706 relative to B5; 50{,}536 have equal edit counts, not necessarily identical transcripts. Against Conf, substitutions and insertions decrease but deletions increase. The shuffled-acoustic control gives CHECK WER 15.99 versus Conf's 15.95 and DEV mean 19.880 versus 19.872.
