# Interpretation and reproduction limits

- This is an identified author manuscript and evidence release, not proof of
  acceptance, a new SOTA, a new beam/PLL/MWER algorithm, or completed institutional
  ethics approval.
- The main evidence is conditional on one backbone, five languages, fixed banks,
  three checkpoints and one fitted selector per configuration. Observed-test
  extensions are not new blind tests. Statistical families do not correct the
  whole development history.
- Global adds a small gain over Conf. Its additional gain over Text was not
  established. An interval crossing zero does not prove equivalence or redundant
  information. Local and objective-control negative results are retained.
- FLEURS uses 30-second inputs and complete references, including 22 long TEST
  recordings. FLEURS results do not invent unverified source/speaker clusters.
- Text needs an extra 1.13 GB model snapshot; the reported peak allocation rises
  from 833 to 2,105 MiB in the measured prototype. A lower WER is not an equal
  resource result. Shared-load timing is not an architecture-inherent guarantee.
- Minimal CPU aggregation, immutable source checks and manuscript compilation are
  validated for this release. Fresh full-corpus ASR/MLM reconstruction is not.
  Exact trained adapters, private caches and raw reference data are absent, so
  this checkout alone cannot reproduce every original experiment or interval.
- Historical provenance paths are retained only where preserving hashed scientific
  assets is useful. They do not grant access to the original machine or data.
